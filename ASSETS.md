# Proton Patrol: art

**Status:** done (Nov 2026). All 25 images are in `assets/` (mascot 320 × 320, badges and logo 256 × 256, transparent WebP), cut from the ChatGPT art package. To replace one, drop in a file with the same name.

Structures and charges inside questions are drawn by code on purpose. Don't make those.

**25 images: 1 logo + 5 menu badges + 2 feature badges + 11 achievement badges + 5 mascot moods (+ study hub card copy of the logo).**

## Mascot: Pip the proton (5 moods, 320 × 320 transparent WebP in `assets/mascot/`)

Pip is a tiny, round, bubblegum-pink ball with a big bold "+" on his tummy, rosy cheeks, two stubby feet and a cheeky grin; he loves hopping from molecule to molecule. Same character on every image.

`pip-idle.webp` (smiling, looking forward), `pip-thinking.webp` (looking up, small "?"), `pip-correct.webp` (closed happy eyes, sparkle), `pip-oops.webp` (swirly eyes, sweat drop, wobbling off a small molecule), `pip-victory.webp` (jumping, stars and confetti).

## Logo

`assets/logo-256.webp` and `games/assets/games/phcharge.webp` (study hub card): Pip in a little patrol cap with a badge, standing on a pH scale strip that runs from pink (acid) to blue (base).
